Signicat is a European digital identity company offering "one point of integration for all your digital identity needs across borders – in every step of the customer journey." Its products cover identity proofing (eID and Wallet Hub, ID document and biometric verification, ReuseID, data verification, ongoing monitoring), authentication (MobileID, passkeys), electronic signing (Electronic Signature API and the Dokobit signature portal), trust orchestration (Signicat Mint no-code workflows, RiskFlow) and digital evidence management, all exposed to developers through the APIs documented at developer.signicat.com.

**Areas:** Company · Digital Identity · Authentication · E-Signature · KYC · Identity Verification · eID · Fraud Prevention
